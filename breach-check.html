Has this password already leaked?

Checks a password against Have I Been Pwned's database of over 800 million breached passwords — without ever sending your actual password anywhere.

⚠ THE ONE EXTERNAL CALL ON THIS SITE

Your password is hashed (SHA-1) right here in your browser first. Only the first 5 characters of that hash ever get sent anywhere — never your actual password, and not even your full hash. This is called k-anonymity, and it's the same method most password managers use for this exact check.
