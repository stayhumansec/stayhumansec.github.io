The password mistake nobody thinks is a big deal. You're probably doing it. 🔑

Here's the spoiler: it's reusing the same password on more than one site. It feels completely harmless — until one random site you signed up for years ago gets breached, and suddenly every other account where you reused that password is at risk too.

The fix doesn't require memorizing anything. Your phone already has a free password manager built in — it can check which of your passwords are reused or already leaked, and generate a new, unique one for each account going forward. Takes a few minutes, and you never have to think about it again.

This is one worth sharing with the people in your family who reuse "the one password" for everything — you know who they are. A five-minute checkup could save them a very bad week.
