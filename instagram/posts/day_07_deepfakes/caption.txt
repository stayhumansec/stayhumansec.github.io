The old deepfake tells don't work anymore. Here's what still does. 🎭

Bad blinking used to give it away. Not anymore — newer models fixed the classic visual tells. Judging a video by "does it look real" alone is a much weaker test than it used to be.

What still holds up: lighting and reflections that don't quite match, hands/edges that warp under close attention, and — most reliably — the actual ask. A request for money or credentials matters more than any visual glitch, because that's the real goal behind most deepfake scams.

When in doubt, verify a second way — call the person back, ask a mutual contact, or check an official source. A fake can't answer a question it's never heard.
