2/ Bad blinking used to give it away. Not anymore — newer models fixed the classic visual tells. "Does it look real" is a much weaker test than it used to be.

3/ What still holds up:
— PHYSICS: lighting/reflections that don't quite match
— HANDS & EDGES: still where most models struggle most
— THE ASK: a request for money/credentials matters more than any glitch

4/ Another tell: you're only being reached one way, with no way offered to confirm through a second channel. A real urgent request usually survives being double-checked.

5/ When in doubt: verify through a channel the other person doesn't control. Call them back, ask a mutual contact, or check an official source. A fake can't answer what it's never heard.
