Every part of it is doing a specific job. 🧵

2/ The sender name is not the sender. Anyone can type "Bank Support" as their display name — that's just a label. The actual email address underneath is what tells you where it really came from.

3/ Most inboxes hide that address by default. Tap the sender name to expand it and check.

4/ The red flags:
— "Dear Customer" instead of your real name
— "Suspended in 24 hours" — manufactured urgency
— Link text that doesn't match where it actually goes when you hover

5/ An unexpected attachment (invoice, shipping label, "document") you never asked for is another common one.

6/ Don't click. Go to the official app or type the site in yourself instead.
