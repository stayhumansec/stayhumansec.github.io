A real phishing email. Broken down, line by line. 🔍

The sender name is not the sender — anyone can type "Bank Support" as their display name. It's the actual email address underneath that tells you where a message really came from, and most inboxes hide it by default unless you tap to expand it.

The red flags: a generic "Dear Customer" greeting instead of your real name, manufactured urgency ("your account will be suspended in 24 hours"), and a link whose visible text doesn't match where it actually goes when you hover over it.

Don't click. Go to the official app or type the site in yourself instead — if something's genuinely wrong, it'll show up there too, with no risky link required.

This is a good one to walk through with anyone in the family who handles their own banking or bills online — knowing what to actually look for makes a huge difference.
