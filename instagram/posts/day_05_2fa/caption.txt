One setting stops most account takeovers cold. Have you turned it on? 🔐

Two-factor authentication (2FA) means proving it's you with something beyond just a password. Your password will leak eventually — breaches happen to companies, not just people — and once it's out there, it gets tried everywhere you might have reused it. 2FA is what stops that stolen password from being enough on its own.

Turn it on in this order: email first (it can reset almost everything else), then banking, then everything else. Pick an authenticator app over SMS where you can — SMS codes can be intercepted through SIM-swap attacks.

Five minutes today. One less way in, forever. Save your backup codes somewhere offline, not on the phone itself.
