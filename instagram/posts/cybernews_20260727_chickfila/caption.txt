Chick-fil-A's rewards app just got hit. Not by a hack. By a stolen password. 🔑

Here's the part that actually matters: Chick-fil-A's own servers weren't broken into. Attackers took a list of usernames and passwords stolen from totally different, unrelated sites, and tried them against the Chick-fil-A One login automatically — for three days straight. Whenever someone had reused a password, it worked.

This is called credential stuffing, and it's the exact mechanism our very first "password mistake" post covers.

Have a Chick-fil-A One account? Open it and check: does your balance and payment method look right? If you've ever reused that password anywhere else, change it now. And turn on 2FA if it's offered — it means a stolen password alone stops being enough.

Not a Chick-fil-A problem. A reused-password problem. The fix is five minutes.
