"Incognito mode makes you anonymous." Nope. 🕵️

Here's what it actually does: stops your browser from saving history, cookies, and autofill on your own device. That's it. Your internet provider, your Wi-Fi network, and the websites you visit can all still see exactly what you're doing — incognito was never built to hide any of that.

It's genuinely useful for what it was designed for — checking someone else's email on a shared computer, or shopping for a gift without your browser suggesting it to whoever you're buying it for. It just isn't the privacy shield a lot of people think it is.

If you want real privacy from your network or provider, that's what a VPN is for — a completely different tool built for a different job.

Worth passing along to anyone in the family who's ever said "it's fine, I'm in incognito mode."
