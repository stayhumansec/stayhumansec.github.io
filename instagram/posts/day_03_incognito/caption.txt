"Incognito mode makes you anonymous." Nope. 🕵️

Here's what it actually does: stops your browser from saving history, cookies, and autofill. That's it. Your ISP, your Wi-Fi network, and the websites you visit can all still see exactly what you're doing — incognito was never built to hide any of that.

If you want real privacy, that's what a VPN is for — not a browser mode. There's a free, open-source pick for one in the Toolkit (link in bio).
