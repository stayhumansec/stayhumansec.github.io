1/ "Incognito mode makes you anonymous."

Nope. Here's what it actually does. 🧵

2/ Incognito mode turns off exactly three things: your browser saving new history, keeping cookies after you close the window, and saving anything you type into forms. That's the whole feature.

3/ What it does NOT do: hide anything from your ISP, your Wi-Fi network, or the websites you visit. That visibility happens at the network/server level — incognito mode never touches it.

4/ If you're on a work or school network, whoever manages it can very likely still see exactly what you looked at, incognito or not.

5/ Want actual privacy from your network or ISP? That's what a VPN is for — a completely different tool than a browser mode.
