"Mom, I need money right now." It sounds exactly like your kid. It might not be. 📞

AI voice cloning now needs as little as 3 seconds of audio — a public video, a voicemail greeting, a livestream clip — to convincingly fake someone's voice on a phone call. Combine that with a panicked, urgent story and a request to send money fast, and it's a scam built to bypass your judgment before you have time to use it.

The tells: pressure to act in minutes, a request for wire transfer, gift cards, or crypto, and being told not to tell anyone else in the family.

The fix is simple and it works: hang up, and call them back directly on a number you already have saved. A cloned voice can't answer a callback it never receives. Some families set up a private "safe word" for exactly this situation.

Please share this with the parents and grandparents in your life — this scam is specifically built to work on people who love someone enough to panic first and think second.
