1/ "Mom, I need money right now."

It sounds exactly like your kid. It might not be. 🧵

2/ AI voice cloning now needs as little as 3 seconds of audio — a public video, a voicemail greeting, a livestream clip — to convincingly fake someone's voice on a phone call.

3/ The tells:
— URGENCY: no time to think or call anyone else first
— PAYMENT: wire transfer, gift cards, or crypto — never traceable
— SECRECY: "don't tell anyone else" so nobody can check the story

4/ The audio itself is often slightly off too — a bit robotic, oddly paced, or conveniently "breaking up" right when details would get specific.

5/ The fix: hang up, and call them back directly on a number you already have saved. A cloned voice can't answer a callback it never receives. Some families set up a private safe word for exactly this.
