1/ "Your package couldn't be delivered. Click here to fix it." 📦

That text is smishing — SMS phishing. Here's how it actually works. 🧵

2/ The wording changes every time. The pattern underneath almost never does:
— a name you trust (bank, delivery company, tax office)
— a manufactured problem that needs fixing right now
— a link that promises to fix it

3/ Why it works: a text feels more urgent and more "official" than an email ever does, and almost nobody's ever been taught to question one the way they question suspicious emails.

4/ Check the sender, not just the message. Real companies text from consistent, verifiable numbers — the same one every time. Scam texts come from random numbers that change constantly.

5/ The fix: never tap the link. Open the company's official app instead, or type their website in yourself. If something's genuinely wrong, it'll be sitting right there too.
